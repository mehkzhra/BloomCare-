BLOOMCARE — PE SCREENING TEST SAMPLES
Synthetic demonstration records. Not real patient reports, treatment targets or clinical diagnoses.

HOW TO USE
Sign in as a demo patient. Open Add health observations.
Select glucose mg/dL and temperature °F. Enter one complete record below.
Expand the additional antenatal/clinical inputs and fill every listed field.
Select Run complete assessment. Use a new record for each case.
These are manual-entry examples, not an import format. Report upload does not run predictions.

VERIFICATION
All three samples returned maternal HIGH and the specified separate PE category in the available local cloud-build models on 26 October 2026. The live app was not queried.
Maternal model: maternal_rf_v0.2. PE model: bloomcare_legacy_pe_v0.7.1.
The maternal HIGH result is intentional: it opens the separate PE stage.
A PE LOW result does not cancel a maternal HIGH result.
HB units are undocumented in the original PE source; enter the demo number only for synthetic testing. Do not label it g/dL or apply it to real patient data.
